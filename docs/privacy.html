Whorl privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Whorl is published by ZenMonster. For support or privacy questions, contact david.jairaj@gmail.com.

Whorl keeps your shortcut settings on your device. The app has no account system, advertising or analytics, and does not connect directly to our servers.

Apps and usage

To let you choose and open apps, Whorl reads the launchable apps installed on your phone, including their names and icons. If you grant optional Usage access, it reads recent app activity to suggest shortcuts. This processing takes place on your device. You choose which suggestions to save and where they appear. Usage access can be revoked in Android Settings.

Contacts and shortcuts

When you choose a person through Android's contact picker, Whorl receives that selected name and phone number. It saves the details as a favourite or shortcut. Whorl does not request access to your full address book or call history. Opening a phone shortcut passes its number to your dialler; you decide whether to call.

Shortcut names, selected apps, website links, phone numbers and any destinations you enter are stored in Whorl's private app storage. Appearance and other settings are stored there too. Whorl does not read your photo library, take photographs or obtain your location: its Camera, Photos and Maps shortcuts open the corresponding apps.

Opening a shortcut may send its number, address or website link to the app you choose. Those apps and services handle the information under their own privacy policies.

Backups

Export creates a plain-text JSON file containing your shortcuts and favourite people. It may include names, numbers, addresses, website links and selected apps. You choose where to save it through Android's file picker. A selected cloud storage provider may upload the file and can access its contents. Whorl does not encrypt exported backups. Store them privately. Restore reads only the file you select. Automatic Android app backup is disabled in Whorl's configuration.

Keeping and deleting data

Saved settings remain until you change or remove them, clear Whorl's app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. You can remove a favourite by holding its name and choosing Remove, or clear an individual shortcut while editing it. Clearing or uninstalling Whorl does not delete backups you exported; delete those separately using the selected storage provider. Whorl does not retain a server copy of your shortcut data.

Contacting support

If you email us, the contents of your message and your email address are sent through the email services you and we use. Do not send passwords, identity documents or private backup files in a support request.

Joining the website’s testing programme is separate from using the app. See testing privacy for signup information.
