Testing details and privacy

26 November 2026

Requesting an invitation

The Android test is open by invitation to people aged 18 or older who use an India Google Play account. To take part, email David Jairaj from the address you use for Google Play. Please plan to stay opted in for 14 consecutive days and share feedback as you use Whorl.

David adds your address to the closed tester list in Google Play Console and replies with instructions. Open the joining link with that same Google account and opt in on Google Play.

How your email is used

David Jairaj operates Whorl under ZenMonster. He uses your address to arrange test access, send joining instructions and follow up on feedback. The invitation link opens your email app. You can review the message before sending it.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Google handles the tester list and David’s Gmail correspondence. GitHub privacy · Google privacy

Leaving the test

You can leave through the Google Play test page. To ask David to remove your address from the tester list or delete your testing correspondence, reply to your invitation or email david.jairaj@gmail.com.

Earlier signups

If you used the earlier verification form, its signup records are held in Cloudflare. Verification links expire after 30 minutes. Unreserved signups expire after 48 hours. Rate-limit records, which may include a keyed hash of a network address, expire within two days. Reserved contact and delivery records are removed within 30 days after the campaign closes, unless needed for an unresolved support request. You can use the same email address above to request removal. Cloudflare privacy

For information about data on your phone, read the Whorl app privacy policy.
