Testing privacy

24 November 2026

Registration is not open yet. This page describes the planned signup process; no signup details are currently being collected here.

What we ask for

Your Google Play account email address, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and confirmation that your Play country is India. We do not ask for your password, date of birth, payment details or identity documents.

Why we need it

We use your address to verify that you control the inbox, reserve one of 20 testing places, add you to Whorl’s Google Play closed test and send your code, joining instructions and essential testing messages. It is not a marketing signup.

Verifying your email does not automatically join the Google Play test. We add verified addresses to the tester list, then send access instructions. You must opt in with that same Google account.

Who handles it

David Jairaj operates Whorl under ZenMonster. The public page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Google handles the closed-test account list and Play code redemption. The signup service uses Cloudflare to store signup records and allocate places; verification and invitation messages are sent through Google from david.jairaj@gmail.com. These services handle their own service logs under their privacy policies.

GitHub privacy · Cloudflare privacy · Google privacy

What stays private

The public counter shows only the number of unclaimed places. Email addresses, verification links and unissued promo codes are never placed in the public GitHub repository or counter. Verification links expire after 30 minutes. Requests are limited to reduce misuse; the service may retain a keyed hash of a network address for those limits.

Retention and removal

Unreserved signup records are removed after 48 hours. Rate-limit records are removed within two days. We retain reserved testers’ contact and delivery records during the campaign, then remove them within 30 days of its closure unless needed for an unresolved support request. You can request removal at david.jairaj@gmail.com. Removing website records does not undo an app entitlement already redeemed on Google Play.

Your free copy

The first 20 eligible people to verify their signup receive one paid-app promo code each, subject to availability. Redeeming it gives you a free copy of Whorl, with no subscription. Keep it after the test. It is tied to the Google account used to redeem it; Google Play availability, account access and device compatibility still apply.

Use the code by 1 February 2027, 4:30 am IST. One place per person. Do not sell or publicly share a code. Remain opted in to the closed test continuously for at least 14 days and share useful feedback. A website signup or APK installation alone does not count as joining the Google Play test.

The app itself

Whorl’s on-device shortcut data is separate from this signup service. Read the app privacy policy.
